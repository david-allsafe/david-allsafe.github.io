~$ cat summary.txt

POS (Point of Sale) application for a small kiosk/cafeteria, built in Python with PyQt5. Authentication with separate profiles (Admin and Seller), seller and product management, sales with automatic VAT calculation, receipt issuing, sales history, and a statistics dashboard with charts (top 5 products, sales by seller).

JSON-based persistence, with unit tests for the core module (system_manager) and documentation generated with pdoc.

~$ cat features.txt

  • Login with two profiles (Admin and Seller) and simulated password recovery
  • Seller and product management (code, name, price, category, image)
  • Sales by category, cart with per-line totals and automatically calculated VAT
  • Sales history and receipt issuing
  • Statistics: pie chart (top 5 products) and bar chart (sales by seller)
